# TASK-100 - a campaign registry, and what is already duplicated

## THE POLICY

`docs/PRODUCTION-SCALE-POLICY.md`: consolidation over proliferation. Maintain
a campaign registry so the system knows its own experiments and cannot create
a duplicate.

## WHAT THE ESTATE LOOKS LIKE NOW

83 HeyReach campaigns, 15 EmailBison campaigns. Exactly ONE of the HeyReach
campaigns was created by Resonate OS; the rest are the client's own history
going back to April. And that history has visible duplication - there are two
campaigns literally named `OMEGA 3` created two minutes apart, three named
variations of `PRODUCTIVE - MARKETING AGENCIES - AUSTRALI...`, and several
`FIXED - PRODUCTIVE - MARKETING AGENCIES - ...` created within minutes of each
other on 2026-07-27.

## WHAT TO BUILD

1. **A registry** at `docs/state/CAMPAIGN-REGISTRY.json`, generated by a
   script, carrying for every campaign at both providers: provider, id, name,
   status, created, lead count, sequence hash where readable, whether Resonate
   created it, and the cohort/hypothesis it serves where known.
2. **A duplicate detector.** Same-or-near name, same sequence hash, same lead
   list, or created within minutes with the same shape. Report what it finds
   in the EXISTING estate - that is the test of whether the detector works.
3. **A pre-creation check** a future campaign build can call: "does a campaign
   for this cohort/hypothesis already exist?" It must be callable and it must
   be TESTED as consumed - the recurring defect in this repository is a thing
   computed correctly that nothing downstream reads.

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Do not delete, rename, pause or modify ANY existing campaign. The client's
  83 campaigns are their history and are not ours to tidy.
- Do not assume a duplicate is a mistake. Two campaigns with one shape may be
  a deliberate geo split. Report, do not judge.
- No unhashed client company names in the registry; campaign NAMES are
  operational metadata and may stay, but anything that identifies a prospect
  must be hashed.

## DELIVERABLE

The script, the registry, the duplicate report over the real estate, and a
test proving the pre-creation check is actually called by something.

## RULES THAT APPLY TO THIS TASK

- Reads only at both providers. No POST/PATCH/PUT/DELETE, no sends, no
  campaign creation or activation, no adding leads.
- **A zero and a wrong lookup are indistinguishable from the outside.** If you
  measure zero of something, prove you read the right field first. Industry
  and headcount were reported at 0% three separate times by a reader looking
  at `sizing`, which is null everywhere, instead of `company_facts`.
- Say what you SAMPLED. `per_page` is accepted and IGNORED on every EmailBison
  route - you get 15 rows whatever you ask for - and offset pagination is
  refused past ~500 pages. A number without its page budget is not
  reproducible.
- Never use `meta.total` as a sent count. Campaign 274 reports 30,411
  scheduled rows and zero of its first 100 pages are sent. Count rows WHERE
  `sent_at` IS PRESENT.
- **Do not quote 8.49%** - unreproduced. **Do not quote any open rate** -
  `open_tracking` is False estate-wide, which is an ABSENT MEASUREMENT and not
  a zero. **Do not count an UNKNOWN as negative** - 53.4% of unknowns are
  correctly unknown.
- INTERESTED may NOT carry a learning claim: 0.44 precision on the old pattern
  set, and the new set is UNMEASURED, which is not the same as good.
  MEETING_INTENT (1.00) and OBJECTION (1.00) may, with recall stated.
- Never weaken, widen or disable a gate, a lint rule or a sender limit to make
  something pass or to gain volume. Fix what produced the bad output.
- Read every test exit code OFF THE PROCESS, never through a pipe.
- No unhashed PII in any tracked file or commit message - no real names,
  domains, emails, profile URLs or reply text. A seat holder is a real person
  too; Claude leaked one yesterday and the guard caught it.
- Separate OBSERVATIONS (with n), HYPOTHESES, and PROVEN LEARNINGS. Leave
  PROVEN LEARNINGS empty if nothing survives a sample-size objection. TASK-059
  left it empty and was right to.
