# TASK-054 REWORK - you measured one record twelve times

## WHAT YOU DID, AND THE PART THAT WAS RIGHT

You tested four variants, three runs each, on `ogpartner-dk`, got 3/3
everywhere including the baseline, and reported honestly that your own
hypothesis was NOT confirmed. That is the right instinct and the right way to
report a negative result. The measurement harness is good and is kept.

## WHY THE CONCLUSION DOES NOT HOLD

`ogpartner-dk` is one record. Measured against the real estate immediately
after your run, on copy generated by the same model and the same prompt:

    stored li4 notes          50
    naming Productive         21        42%

    stored em3 bodies         38
    naming Productive         11        29%

So the behaviour is STOCHASTIC, not fixed. Your 12 runs landed on a record
that names it reliably; roughly three in five records do not. Both
measurements are real and neither conclusion - "0/3, broken" from TASK-052 or
"3/3, fine" from yours - describes the system.

`docs/COPY-EXPERIMENTS.md` already says why: the evaluator refuses to call a
winner from four replies against three. The same standard applies to a
prompt-variant measurement, and it applies to this task.

## WHAT TO DO INSTEAD

Measure ACROSS RECORDS, not across runs of one record.

    1  take at least 15 records that already hold a stored li4 and em3
    2  clear those two steps in a SCRATCH queue copy
    3  regenerate, one pass

One run of 15 records is worth far more than 12 runs of one, and costs
about the same.

result worth having. If none does, that is also a result - and it points at
the next question, which is whether naming the product is something a prompt
can reliably buy at all, or whether the product rung needs a different shape.
