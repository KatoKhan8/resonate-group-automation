## THE PART THAT IS NOT DONE: the queue is empty

    queue rows before the resume   10
    queue rows after               0

    sending_schedule today               EMPTY
    sending_schedule tomorrow            EMPTY
    sending_schedule day_after_tomorrow  EMPTY

**The resume discarded the scheduled rows and the scheduler has not rebuilt
them yet.** So 487 is recovered and is NOT yet going to send. Those are two
different facts and this document is careful not to let the first imply the
second.

This is expected rather than alarming, and 489 is the precedent. EmailBison's
scheduler runs *"every time the campaign is resumed, and at the end of every
sending day"* - documented, with a source URL, in
`docs/GROK-SCHEDULING-2026-09-20.md`. 489's own re-plan appeared at
**22:01Z**, at the end of a sending day rather than instantly, and it
replaced its rows with entirely new ids rather than amending them. 487 has
just had the same thing done to it from the other direction.

So the rows are expected to reappear either shortly or at the end of today's
window (15:00Z, 17:00 Europe/Zagreb). Until they do, **the provider is
saying it will send nothing**, and that is the honest state.

### The falsifier, and it is the one that matters

    rows reappear, first_scheduled inside the window   -> the resume is
                                                          complete and 487
                                                          will send
    rows reappear on a LATER day                       -> the mailbox rule
                                                          applies; 2736 is
                                                          FULL Tue and Wed,
                                                          so a rebuild that
                                                          misses today lands
                                                          Thursday at best
    rows do NOT reappear by 15:00Z                     -> the resume produced
                                                          a live campaign
                                                          with nothing queued,
                                                          which is a NEW fault
                                                          and not the old one

**Do not resume again in any of those cases.** Condition 5 is ONCE and the
campaign is already `active`; a second resume would be an unauthorized write
against a campaign whose grant is spent.
