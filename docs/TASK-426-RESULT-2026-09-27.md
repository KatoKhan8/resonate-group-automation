# TASK-426 — `bisonfactory.stage()` hands the sequence gate all five inputs

**Branch `worktree-agent-afbc43865ecef2efd`, head `42878c86`, branched from master
`1f4d464c`. NOT MERGED.** Every number below was measured on this branch on

## THE DEFECT, AND WHY IT WAS FATAL

`6fa49014` ("TASK-321 PARTIAL") wired `sequencegate.check` into `bisonfactory`
and called it with ONE of its five inputs. The gate refuses when no
qualification is supplied — *"absence is refused rather than read as
qualified"* — so **every `stage()` refused for every input** from 2026-09-26
15:27. It failed CLOSED. Nothing reached a provider, and no campaign could be
staged.

## WHERE EACH INPUT COMES FROM

| input | source | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `sequence` | the lead's approved copy, keyed by cadence step | unchanged |
| `qualification` | `qualify.state_of(record)`, carried per lead on the plan | the canonical resolver `enrich` already uses; no second representation |
| `facts` | `packfacts.pack_for(record)` | the same identity-checked pack `_copylint_batch` uses; only the key name is translated (`snippet` → `text`) |
| `capability` | `cadence.product_words(contact, config)` | the client's own capability sentence, resolved by persona — the same sentence rung 3 renders |
| `batch_capabilities` | **not supplied, deliberately** | the gate's own warning reports it as unchecked; see below |

**Nothing is defaulted and no placeholder is passed.** A record nobody
qualified resolves to `not_processed`, which the gate refuses.

### `qualification`: why the ICP verdict, and why it refuses nothing real

Measured on `work/queue.jsonl`: 1,582 records, 1,099 carry generated copy, and
of those **only 55 also carry research** — and research is what the batch copy
lint requires before a push can reach the sequence gate at all. Of those 55:

    qualified   53
    rejected     2
    no verdict   0

So threading the verdict refuses nothing that is legitimately stageable today,
and it refuses the two that a campaign should never have held. The 1,031
copy-bearing records with no verdict are `domains`-lane records with **zero
research**; they are already refused one gate earlier, by `_refuse_copylint`.

### `batch_capabilities`: an unchecked question is reported, not hidden

That check asks whether the copy engine's stage D chose a capability per lead
or defaulted to one, and it fails when five or more leads share a single
distinct value. **There is no stage D on this path.** The capability here is a
deterministic function of the contact's persona and the client's file, and
`config/clients/productive.yaml` configures two personas — so a single-persona
cohort legitimately shares one capability.

Measured on `work/campaigns.jsonl`: **five staged campaigns are single-persona**
(9, 10, 10, 10 and 5 sendable leads). Supplying the list would refuse all five
for a defect they do not have. The gate already has the honest answer for a
caller that cannot answer the question — `batch_capabilities is None` warns that
stage D's choosing was NOT checked — and that warning is true here. A test pins
the warning so the silence cannot be mistaken for "stage D is choosing".

## PER LEAD, NOT ONCE PER CAMPAIGN

The call site checked the FIRST lead and staged the rest, on the grounds that
"the sequence is campaign-level". It is not: the provider sequence is a template
of merge fields and every lead carries its own words, its own account's facts
and its own company's verdict. Lead one passing proved nothing about lead fifty.
The refusal now names the lead as well as the step.

## `sequencegate.BLOCKING_QUALIFICATIONS` EXTENDED, NOT WEAKENED

It gained `REJECTED`, `REVIEW`, `NOT_PROCESSED` and `CLASSIFIED` — the
vocabulary of `qualify.state_of`. Without them the staging path would have
handed the gate a real qualification the gate was **incapable of refusing**:
`rejected` would have read as acceptable and the `qualified` check could have
refused nothing but a `None`. That is the same defect this tuple was corrected
for once before (`INSUFFICIENT_DATA` vs `INSUFFICIENT`). No value that was
refused before becomes accepted, and the copy engine's own vocabulary
(`QUALIFIED_RICH`, `QUALIFIED_THIN`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `UNQUALIFIED`) is
untouched.

## FIXTURES COMPLETED — NO ASSERTION WEAKENED

Seven staging fixtures described a record that cannot exist: copy and research
but no ICP verdict (see the measurement above). Each gained
`qualification.verdict.icp_status = icp.QUALIFIED`, written explicitly rather
than scored for the reason `tests.base.qualify_everything` documents.

Three fixtures also wrote every follow-up as `body for emN`. Those reduce to the
single content word "body" — the step key is not a content word — so two
follow-ups scored **100% overlap** and the gate is right to call that one
argument repeated five times. The follow-up sentences now come from
`tests/packfixture.py`, so the three modules cannot drift apart again, and two
literal assertions in `test_threaded_sequence` were pointed at the helper rather
than restated.

**The five tests in `tests/test_lead_writes_respect_the_killswitch` are
byte-identical to master's.**
