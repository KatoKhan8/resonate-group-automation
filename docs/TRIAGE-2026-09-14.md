# Triage, 2026-09-14: what blocks a lane and what does not

Classified on the operator's own scale.

    P0  could contact the wrong person, duplicate a send, violate
        suppression, fabricate a claim, or corrupt provider state
        -> BLOCK the affected lane
    P1  materially damages copy, cadence or outcome correctness
        -> fix quickly, unaffected production continues
    P2  analytics or optimisation
        -> queue it, do NOT block production
