**Production consequence:** Unclear without further investigation. Either:
- The test fixture is incomplete (needs more fields in the record for cadence.build to work)

## test_referral (1 failure)

**Verdict: REAL DEFECT**

**Evidence:** Test `test_a_plain_hand_off_holds_the_referrer` expects that after a referral, the referrer (contact "acme-champ") is paused. The test calls `self.champ(rec).get("paused")` and expects it to be truthy, but it returns None.

The test docstring says: "A referral is not permission to keep writing to the person who handed us on. `reply.on_referral` holds them, which is a pause a person can lift - not the end of the sequence."

The referral handling is not pausing the referrer. This is a regression in the referral logic.

**Production consequence:** After a person refers us to a colleague, the referrer continues to receive messages. This is the exact behavior the referral rule is meant to prevent. A person who said "talk to Sarah Jones" would get another message from us instead of being paused. This damages trust and violates the ACCOUNT-OUTREACH.md rule that a referral is an introduction, not permission to keep writing to the referrer.

**Fix:** Investigate `reply.on_referral` or the referral handling path in `src/referral.py` or `src/reply.py`. The referrer should be paused (not stopped) after a referral is recorded.

## test_the_account_is_the_unit_of_outreach (3 failures)

**Verdict: REAL DEFECT**

**Evidence:** All three failures are related to account fatigue not being detected:
1. `test_the_check_returns_the_reason_when_the_company_is_past_policy`: `_account_fatigue()` returns None instead of `HELD_ACCOUNT_FATIGUE` for a record with `limit + 2` touches.
2. `test_a_company_past_its_weekly_limit_is_held`: The verdict has `["held:verification_unknown"]` instead of `["held:account_fatigue"]`.
3. `test_it_is_asked_before_any_content_work`: Same issue - verification_unknown instead of account_fatigue.

The `_account_fatigue` function calls `fatigue.account_check()` and expects it to return `state: BLOCK` when the company has exceeded its weekly limit. But it's not returning BLOCK, so `_account_fatigue` returns None. Then the verification check runs and returns HELD_VERIFICATION_UNKNOWN because the test record has no verification evidence.

The test record created by `touched()` has events but no verification evidence. The account fatigue check should catch this BEFORE the verification check, but it's not detecting the fatigue condition.

**Production consequence:** A company that has exceeded its weekly touch limit is not being held for account fatigue. Instead, it's being held for verification_unknown (or passing through if it has verification). This violates the ACCOUNT-OUTREACH.md rule that "the account is the unit of outreach" and the account-level limits are enforced. A company could be worked past policy without being stopped.

**Fix:** Investigate `fatigue.account_check()` to understand why it's not returning BLOCK for a record with `limit + 2` touches. The function may be checking the wrong thing, or the test record may not have the right structure. The account fatigue check must happen before verification checks, as the test comment says: "A held account should cost no lint and no claim check."
